# ios contract discovery

Use endly_service_info with service and optional action through MCP. Or inspect the live CLI contract with `endly -s=ios -a=<action>`. Read `service/testing/runner/ios/README.md` in a matching Endly checkout for maintained service documentation.

Available source-declared actions: artifact, attach, build, capture-start, capture-stop, cleanup, close, destination-register, destination-release, device-lease, device-list, device-release, doctor, install, launch, open, repl, run, server-start, server-stop, simulator-start, simulator-stop, terminate, test, uninstall.
