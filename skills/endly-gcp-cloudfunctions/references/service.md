# gcp/cloudfunctions contract discovery

Use endly_service_info with service and optional action through MCP. Or inspect the live CLI contract with `endly -s=gcp/cloudfunctions -a=<action>`. Read `service/system/cloud/gcp/cloudfunctions/README.md` in a matching Endly checkout for maintained service documentation.

Available source-declared actions: call, delete, deploy, get, list.
