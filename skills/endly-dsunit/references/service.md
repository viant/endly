# dsunit contract discovery

Use endly_service_info with service and optional action through MCP. Or inspect the live CLI contract with `endly -s=dsunit -a=<action>`. Read `service/testing/dsunit/README.md` in a matching Endly checkout for maintained service documentation.

Available source-declared actions: checkSchema, compare, create, dump, expect, freeze, init, mapping, prepare, query, register, script, sequence, sql.
